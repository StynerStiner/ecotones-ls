Native Problems Need Native Solutions

Watersheds, fire-prone slopes, restored creeks — these aren’t problems to be solved by a faster machine. They’re systems that have done their job for thousands of years. When something breaks, the answer is rarely synthetic. It’s slower attention, native ecology, and people who’ll come back next season to see what took. The ecotone is the edge between two ecosystems — where the most life happens. That’s where we work: the seam between what people need and what land can give back. Done right, both flourish.
