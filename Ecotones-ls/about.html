Who we are

Long-View Stewardship
For Working Land

Ecotones is a Northern California land stewardship company. We work across defensible space, biochar, native plant propagation, pond construction, and stream restoration — with bilingual EN/ES crews and quote-based scoping. We work at the pace of the land, and we come back to make sure the work took.

Meet The Founder

Robert Symens-Bucher

Founder & Lead Steward

Robert founded Ecotones to do the kind of land work he wanted to see — honest scoping, real follow-through, and bilingual crews who treat each watershed on its own terms.
