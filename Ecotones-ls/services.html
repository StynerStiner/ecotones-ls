Our Services

The Services We Offer To
Our Clients

Stream Restoration

Bank stabilization, riparian native planting, and watershed work — like our Canticle Creek case study.

Defensible Space

Fire-wise zone planning around homes and outbuildings — fire-resilient native plantings included.

Biochar Production

On-site biochar from forest biomass — locking carbon back into the ground as soil amendment.

Bilingual Trainings

Workshops in English & Spanish for crews, landowners, and partner organizations — meeting people where they steward.

Pond Construction

Design and construction of ponds — built for habitat, water storage, and the species your land supports.

Fuels Reduction

Brush clearance and ladder-fuel removal — reducing wildfire risk while keeping ecological structure intact.

Rooted In
Northern California

We're based in Oakland and work across Northern California — from coastal watersheds to inland forests. Every property is different, but the approach is the same: scope honestly, work at the pace of the land, and come back to make sure the work took.
